FAQ

Straight answers.

Why not just install an app?

Apps charge every month, load scripts on every page, and only do what they allow. A custom build costs once and fits your brand exactly. If an app is truly better for you, I'll say so.

Will it work with my theme?

Yes. I build in Liquid, which every Shopify theme uses — on a copy of your theme first.

Can my team edit it later?

Yes. Text, colors, products and prices are settings in the Shopify theme editor.

How much does it cost?

A fixed price, agreed before I start. Bundle pages start at $800.

Where are you based?

Agadir, Morocco. I work with stores in the US and Europe, in English or French.

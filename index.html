FAQ

Straight answers.

Why not just install an app?

Apps charge monthly, add scripts to every page, and only do what they allow. A custom build costs once and fits your brand.

Will it work with my theme?

Yes. It's built in Liquid, which every Shopify theme uses — on a copy of your theme first.

Can my team edit it later?

Yes. Text, colors, products and prices are theme editor settings.

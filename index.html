Questions merchants ask me

Good questions. Straight answers.

Why not just install a bundle app?

Apps work — I built one. But you pay every month, their scripts load on every page, and you can only customize what the app allows. A custom build costs once, fits your brand exactly and loads faster. If an app really is the better choice for your store, I'll tell you.

Will this work with my theme?

Yes. I work directly in Liquid, the language every Shopify theme is written in, so it works with Dawn, paid themes and custom themes. I build on a copy of your theme first, so we find out before anything goes live.

Can my team change it later without you?

That's the point. Texts, colors, prices, products and sizes are settings you change in the Shopify theme editor — the same place you already edit your store.

What happens when my theme updates?

Everything I add lives in its own sections and snippets, and I document where it sits. When you update your theme, it carries over cleanly instead of being tangled into files Shopify replaces.

How much does it cost?

A fixed price you agree to before I start. Bundle pages start from $800. Other features are quoted after a short call, once I know exactly what you need. No hourly surprises.

Where are you, and how do we communicate?

I'm based in Agadir, Morocco, and work with stores in the US and Europe. You get updates in plain language, by email or whatever you already use, and a preview link you can check any time.

What does the AI Gift Concierge actually use?

Only your own products and prices — it never invents a product. It suggests, the shopper decides, and your checkout stays exactly as it is. I'm launching it with two pilot stores first, at a reduced price in exchange for honest feedback.
